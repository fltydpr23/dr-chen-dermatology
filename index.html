About Dr Chen

Two Decades of Skin Expertise, Now in Private Practice

Dr Chen Qiping is a Ministry of Health-accredited Consultant Dermatologist with over 20 years of medical experience spanning Singapore, China, the United Kingdom, and the United States.

Before establishing her own clinic, Dr Chen served as a Consultant Dermatologist at the National Skin Centre and Visiting Consultant at Khoo Teck Puat Hospital. She is a Fellow of the Academy of Medicine, Singapore, and has published over 20 peer-reviewed research papers in international dermatology journals. She has also co-authored dermatology reference books in English and Chinese, and has appeared on television, radio, and in print media to educate the public on skin health.

Her subspecialty interests — skin cancers and pigmentary disorders — place her among a small group of dermatologists in Singapore with dedicated expertise in these areas. She practices with a calm, methodical approach and believes every skin concern deserves individualised, evidence-based care.
